OUR STORY

Breakfast Has Always Been More Than a Meal.

At Pancake & Breakfast House, we believe a great morning starts with more than what is on your plate. It starts with the people around the table, the smell of fresh coffee and the feeling of having nowhere else to be.

What began with a simple love for homemade breakfast grew into a place where families, friends and neighbors could come together and enjoy food made with care.

From fluffy pancakes and golden waffles to hearty breakfast classics, every dish is prepared with thoughtfully selected ingredients and the same attention to detail we would give our own family.
