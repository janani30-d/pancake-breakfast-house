GOOD TO KNOW

A Few Things You Might Be Wondering.

Planning a breakfast gathering should feel simple. Find answers to common questions about our catering packages, event planning, and booking process.

We recommend enquiring as early as possible so we can discuss your event details and catering requirements. Booking timelines depend on the event date, guest count, and package availability.
Guest count requirements may vary depending on the catering package and event type. Contact our team with your estimated number of guests to discuss a suitable option.
Let us know your preferred menu, event style, and budget. Our team can discuss available customization options and help you choose a package that suits your gathering.
Please share any dietary preferences or allergies when submitting your enquiry. Our team can confirm which options are available and discuss relevant food preparation considerations.
Delivery and setup arrangements depend on your event location and selected package. Include your venue details in the enquiry so we can confirm the available services.
Start by submitting the catering enquiry form with your event details. Our team can discuss your requirements, confirm availability, and explain the next steps for securing your booking.
Please contact our team as soon as possible if your guest count changes. We can review the updated numbers and explain any changes to the package, pricing, or arrangements.
